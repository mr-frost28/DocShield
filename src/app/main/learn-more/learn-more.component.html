Learn More About DocShield

A secure, serverless platform for storing, sharing, and verifying academic and professional documents.

Why Choose DocShield?

📂 Secure Storage

Your documents are encrypted and stored safely in the cloud with AWS S3.

🔗 Easy Sharing

Share documents with recruiters, institutions, or peers using secure links.

✅ Instant Verification

Employers and universities can instantly verify authenticity using blockchain-backed checks.

⚡ Serverless Architecture

Powered by AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, and Cognito for scalability and cost efficiency.

How It Works

  1. Upload: Students and job seekers upload their academic or professional documents.
  2. Store: Files are securely stored and linked to your verified identity.
  3. Share: Generate secure shareable links with fine-grained permissions.
  4. Verify: Recruiters/universities validate instantly with blockchain checks.
